④ Parallel Test Channels

What this demonstrates: a payload with six sensor packages is validated in one parallel pass instead of six serial ones — every channel's value is checked against its limit in real time, a failed channel gets an AI-drafted triage, and the Data Trust panel proves the numbers before anyone acts on them. Toggle "data fault" and a channel reads PASS but is quarantined because its instrument is out of cal — product-green, data-red, held anyway. That is "implement automated data-quality checks, validation rules, and monitoring… to ensure our quality data is provably trustworthy," made literal.
