What this demonstrates for the interview: the operator never touches a spreadsheet — they do the physical work and sign off; the metric, the yield, the capability index, and the drift alert that catches the trend before any part goes out of spec are all generated from that one action. That is "translate physical manufacturing processes into actionable digital dashboards," and it is the same eWI→capture→scorecard shape whether the backend is my AWS/Python stack or Anduril's Foundry/Databricks. The Data Trust panel adds the rigor layer: a frozen gauge, a sensor glitch, or an out-of-cal instrument gets quarantined and held out of the Cpk/FPY/chart — so the scorecard is provably trustworthy, not just green.
