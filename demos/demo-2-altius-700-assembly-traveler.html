④ Data Trust · record checks ✓ TRUSTED

What this demonstrates: the operator follows a visual guide and signs off physical stations — bonding a composite wing, torquing a shear pin, balancing a pusher prop. Every sign-off writes the build thread, updates first-pass yield, and flags a station that measures out of tolerance for rework before the airframe moves downstream. That is "translate physical manufacturing processes into actionable digital dashboards," on the exact product family the Atlanta site builds. The Data Trust panel adds the rigor layer: toggle "S/N not in ERP" and every sign-off becomes an orphan record — the traveler reads complete but the build can't be released until the serial is reconciled. Provably trustworthy, not just done.
